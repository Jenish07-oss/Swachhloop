HOW GREEN POINTS ARE CALCULATED

Verified Multiplier Rates

You earn Green Points based on the waste stream type and verified weight (KG) of waste collected by municipal drivers.

🟢 WET WASTE 2 Points / KG
🔵 DRY WASTE 6 Points / KG
🟡 E-WASTE 20 Points / KG
🔴 RESIDUAL 1 Point / KG
Calculation Example:
Wet Waste    4 KG × 2 pts/kg = 8 Points
Dry Waste    3 KG × 6 pts/kg = 18 Points
Total Verified Waste = 7 KG Total Green Points = 26 Points
Verification Guarantee: Points are awarded only after the collected waste is verified by municipal drivers and citizens. The final points are calculated using the verified KG, not the initial estimated KG.
