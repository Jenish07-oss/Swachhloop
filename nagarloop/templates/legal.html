At {{ BRAND }}, we are committed to protecting citizen privacy and ensuring transparent municipal operations. Our privacy practices adhere to Indian data protection principles.

Key Privacy Principles

  • 1. Data Minimization: We only collect information essential for pickup logistics — household name, address/landmark, and optional phone number.
  • 2. Purpose-Bound Location Data: Geographic coordinates are used strictly to optimize municipal collection van routes and reduce fuel emissions.
  • 3. Zero Commercial Monetization: Citizen waste logs, photos, and personal records are never sold or shared with commercial marketing third parties.
  • 4. Encrypted Digital Manifests: QR code manifests generate tamper-evident chain-of-custody tokens for certified recycling facilities.
  • 5. Immutable Audit Logs: All collection status transitions, citizen verification votes, and admin resolutions are permanently recorded in system audit logs.
  • 6. Citizen Data Rights: Citizens have full access to view their historical pickup ledger, Green Points records, and request data rectification anytime.
{% elif page_type == 'rewards' %}

{{ BRAND }} Green Rewards incentivizes segregation at source. Households and residential societies earn verifiable Green Points for every segregated pickup.

Green Points Rules & Valuation

  • 1. Transparent Scoring Formula: Every verified pickup earns 20 Base Points + floor(Bin Quality Score / 10). A 100-score bin earns 30 Green Points.
  • 2. Multi-Stream Quality Multiplier: Segregating hazardous E-Waste or high-purity dry recyclables unlocks bonus points on the monthly ledger.
  • 3. Society Collective Podiums: Residential societies aggregate points from all member flats to compete on the Ward Green Champions Leaderboard.
  • 4. Civic & Merchant Redemption: Redeem accumulated Green Points for municipal property tax rebates, compost bags, or local merchant discounts.
  • 5. Anti-Fraud & Two-Way Verification: Points are only credited once the physical collection is confirmed by the citizen or verified at the circular facility.
  • 6. Quarterly Sustainability Badges: Top 10% segregators receive official municipal certificates and green champion society plaques.
